2. Detailed Integration Mechanics

  • Structure-from-Motion (GLOMAP & COLMAP): Integrated into crates/engine/src/sfm_glomap.rs to convert raw video keyframes into calibrated 6DoF camera poses without requiring proprietary software.
  • Metal / WebGPU Splat Rasterization (Brush): Integrated into crates/engine/src/splat_trainer.rs to perform differentiable rasterization natively on Apple Silicon GPUs over Metal.
  • Web Browser Viewport & Exporter (SuperSplat & Three.js): Powers packages/web-viewer/, displaying up to millions of Gaussians at 60 FPS in WebGL2 and WebGPU.
  • iOS AR Companion Pose Streaming (MonoGS & ARKit): Powers apps/mobile/SplatCatCompanion/ using ARKit LiDAR to capture real-time camera matrices and stream keyframes via WebSockets.
